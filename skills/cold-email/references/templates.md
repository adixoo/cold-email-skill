# Cold outreach templates

Structures based on the Context + Relevance + Proof + Small Ask formula. Rewrite every line in the sender's own voice and replace every `[placeholder]` with something real.

---

## 1 - Founder (join a startup)

**For:** early-stage startups, internships or full-time roles where the founder is the decision maker.

**Subject:** Interested in building with [Company]

> Hi [Name],
>
> [One specific observation about their product, feature, launch or the problem they solve - and why it caught your attention.]
>
> I'm a [your role] and I've built [relevant project] with [1-2 relevant technologies]. [One line on a related internship, client project or result.] That seems close to what you're working on at [Company].
>
> Are you open to bringing on [developers / designers / role] right now?
>
> Portfolio: [link]
> GitHub: [link]
>
> [Your Name]

**Why it works:** it ties the sender's experience to the founder's product and gives them something concrete to check.
**Make it stronger:** the opening observation is everything - make it something only someone who used the product would say.

## 2 - Role application (recruiter / hiring manager)

**For:** an advertised internship or full-time role.

**Subject:** [Role] at [Company] | [Most relevant skill or project]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw the [Role] opening at [Company] and wanted to reach out to you directly.
>
> I work with [skill 1] and [skill 2] - both in the job description - and on [project / past role] I [specific contribution or measurable result].
>
> You can see the work here: [portfolio or GitHub link].
>
> Is the role still open? I'd be glad to be considered.
>
> [Your Name]
> LinkedIn: [link] · Portfolio: [link]

**Why it works:** the reader immediately knows which role, why the sender fits, and where to verify it.
**Make it stronger:** pick skills from the job description, not the sender's full stack.

## 3 - Referral (employee at target company)

**For:** someone who works at a company where the sender wants to apply.

**Subject:** Quick question about [Role] at [Company]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm applying for the [Role] role at [Company] ([job link]) and saw that you're on the [team] team.
>
> My background is in [relevant skills], especially [skill the role needs most]. Most recently I [relevant project or achievement].
>
> Portfolio for context: [link]. [Resume attached.]
>
> If my profile looks like a fit, would you be comfortable referring me? Totally fine if not.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

**Why it works:** the employee gets everything they need to judge the profile without searching.
**Make it stronger:** confirm the role is still open and always include the job link.

## 4 - Client pitch (freelance / services)

**For:** business owners who might need web, app, AI automation, design or other services.

**Subject:** An idea for [Business/Product]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I was looking at [Business/Product] and noticed [a real, specific problem or improvement opportunity].
>
> I do [specific service], and I think [proposed solution] could help with [realistic business outcome].
>
> I did something similar for [past client or project] - here it is: [link].
>
> Would you be open to me sending over a few quick ideas for [Business/Product]?
>
> [Your Name]

**Why it works:** it opens with their business problem, not a sales pitch.
**Make it stronger:** research first. Never invent problems or promise numbers you can't support.

---

## 5 - Short DM (LinkedIn / X / WhatsApp)

**For:** founders, recruiters and professionals who are active on social.

> Hey [Name], [one specific line about their product, post or work].
>
> I'm a [your role] working with [relevant skills] - I recently built [one relevant project/result].
>
> Would love to connect and see if there's a fit with your team. Portfolio: [link]

**Why it works:** readable in a few seconds and still has context and proof.
**Make it stronger:** for character-limited connection requests, cut to the first line plus the ask; share links after they accept.

---

## 6 - Follow-up

**For:** no reply to the first message. Send 3-5 business days later, as a reply in the same thread.

**Subject:** Re: [Original subject]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on my note about [role / project / idea].
>
> I'm still keen because [short, genuine reason], and I think my work on [relevant skill or project] could be useful to [team / Company].
>
> Is this relevant for your team right now?
>
> [Your Name]

**Why it works:** a light reminder, no guilt, no repeated pitch.
**Make it stronger:** add one new thing (a new project, a fresh idea) instead of only "bumping". After one more final check-in, move on.
